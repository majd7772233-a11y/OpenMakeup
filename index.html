Open Makeup

A little magic, powered by open technology.

Open Makeup is an experimental real-time virtual beauty studio. Try makeup styles, preview colors, and explore face-shaping controls through an interactive camera experience — all inside a sleek, glass-inspired interface.

◉ MediaPipe Face MeshTracks facial landmarks in the camera frame so effects can follow the face in real time.
✧ WebGL + Three.jsGPU-powered rendering and shader effects draw the virtual makeup and visual layers.
⌁ On-device previewThe bundled demo processes the live preview locally; camera access is requested by Android.
◇ Android 6.0+Designed for Android 6.0 Marshmallow and newer, subject to device WebView and graphics support.

MediaPipe is Google's open-source framework for building machine-learning perception features. This app uses its Face Mesh solution together with WebGL-based rendering. Results can vary by lighting, camera quality, device performance, and installed Android System WebView.
